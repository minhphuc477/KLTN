## Abstract / Core Idea
— The focus of this survey is on research in applying evolutionary and other metaheuristic search algorithms to automatically generating content for games, both digital a nd non-digital (such as board games). The term search-based procedural content generation is proposed as the name for this emerging ﬁeld, which at present is growing quickly. A taxonomy for procedural content generation is devised, centering on what kind of content is generated, how the content is represented and how the quality/ﬁtness of the content is evaluated; sear ch- based procedural content generation in particular is situa ted within this taxonomy. This article also contains a survey of all published papers known to the authors in which game content is generated through search or optimisation, and ends with a n overview of important open research problems.
