Toledo Universidade de São Paulo São Carlos, São Paulo, Brazil claudio@icmc.usp.br ABSTRACT Procedural Content Generation (PCG) methods are valuable tools to speed up the game development process. Moreover, PCG may also be present in games as features, such as the procedural dungeon generation (PDG) in Moonlighter (Digital Sun, 2018). This paper introduces an extended version of an evolutionary dungeon gener- ator by incorporating a MAP-Elites population. Our dungeon levels are discretized with rooms that may have locked-door missions and enemies within them. We encoded the dungeons through a tree structure to ensure the feasibility of missions. We performed computational and user feedback experiments to evaluate our PDG approach. They show that our approach accurately converges al- most the whole MAP-Elite population for most executions. Finally, players’ feedback indicates that they enjoyed the generated levels, and they could not indicate an algorithm as a level generator.
